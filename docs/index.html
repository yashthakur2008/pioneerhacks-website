How it works

Four layers from idea to demo.

01 / Signal

Students arrive with rough ideas.

The site routes beginners, designers, engineers, and founders into clear tracks before the event starts.

02 / Match

Teams form around momentum.

Fast prompts, mentor office hours, and club leads help people find collaborators without awkward chaos.

03 / Build

The room turns ideas into demos.

Workshops, judging criteria, and milestone check-ins keep the hackathon intense but navigable.

04 / Ship

Every team leaves with a story.

Final demos emphasize outcomes, not just code: users reached, problems solved, and next steps.
