FAQ

Clear answers, no fluff.

Who can attend?

PioneerHacks VI is open to high school students. Cupertino students are organizing it, but the event is built for all motivated high school builders.

When is it?

We are targeting mid-to-late October. The final date and venue will be announced as soon as school logistics are locked.

Do I need experience?

No. Come with curiosity. We will support first-time hackers, designers, founders, and experienced builders.

Do I need a team?

No. You can sign up solo and join team formation before hacking starts.
